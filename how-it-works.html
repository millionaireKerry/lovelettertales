How It Works

A subscription to Love Letter Tales is simple, beautiful, and entirely screen-free. Here's everything you need to know.

1

Choose Your Story

Browse our collections and pick the tale that calls to you. Each collection is set in a different historical era, with its own world, voice, and cast of characters.

2

Subscribe

Choose a monthly or annual plan. You can subscribe for yourself or gift a subscription to someone special. All plans include free delivery across the UK.

3

Receive Your Letters

Twice a month, a beautifully crafted story letter arrives at your door by Royal Mail. Each one continues the narrative, drawing you deeper into the tale.

✻ ✻ ✻

What's Inside Each Letter?

Every letter is a chapter in an unfolding story written in the voice of a fictional character from a carefully researched historical period. The letters are printed on beautiful, heavyweight paper with period-appropriate design details. They feel like genuine correspondence from another time.

Each letter is an instalment to savour over a cup of tea. Keep the letters as the story unfolds, and return to your favourite moments whenever you like.

You'll receive 2 letters per month delivered by Royal Mail on the 1st and 15th. Each letter picks up where the last one left off, so the story builds with every delivery.

A Story That Grows With You

Unlike a book you might read in a weekend, our stories unfold slowly the way real letters do. You live with the characters. You wait for the next instalment. You find yourself wondering what happened between the letters. It's a reading experience unlike anything else.

Rose Ashworth and Clara Wren each have 24 letters, unfolding over 12 months. Shorter gift plans introduce the recipient to the opening part of their chosen story; a 12-month plan includes the whole collection.

Gifting a Subscription

Love Letter Tales makes a truly unforgettable gift. You choose the collection, the plan, and the start date and we take care of the rest. We can include a personalised gift note with the first letter, so your loved one knows who sent this beautiful surprise.

Gift subscriptions are available as 3, 6 or 12-month plans with a one-off payment.

Perfect for: Birthdays, anniversaries, Mother's Day, Christmas, or simply because someone you love deserves something beautiful in their letterbox.

Delivery & Packaging

All letters are delivered by Royal Mail across the UK. They arrive in a flat, letterbox-friendly envelope no trips to the sorting office required. Our packaging is made from recycled and recyclable materials, because we believe beautiful things shouldn't cost the earth.
