How Gifting Works

Three simple steps to give someone a gift they'll treasure.

1

You Choose

Pick a story and gift length 3, 6, or 12 months and complete your purchase.

2

We Email You

We send you a beautiful printable gift card immediately perfect for tucking into a birthday card or placing under the tree.

3

They Receive Letters

Your recipient chooses their story and enters their address. The first letters arrive within 7 days, then 2 every month posted on the 1st and 15th.
