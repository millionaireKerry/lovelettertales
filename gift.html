How Gifting Works

Gifting a Love Letter Tales subscription is simple and thoughtful. Here's what happens when you place a gift order:

Step 1: Choose a monthly or annual plan and complete your purchase.

Step 2: We'll send you a beautiful printable gift card (by email) that you can give to your recipient — perfect for tucking into a birthday card or placing under the tree.

Step 3: Your recipient uses the gift card to choose their story collection and enter their delivery address. The first letter arrives within a week.

Who Is It For?

Love Letter Tales makes a wonderful gift for anyone who loves reading, history, beautiful stationery, or simply receiving something lovely in the post. It's especially perfect for:
