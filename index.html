A few words from Rose

“A letter arrived this morning, quite unexpectedly. It was not from you, nor from Aunt Cecily, nor from any hand I recognised.”

From Letter 1 of Rose Ashworth · Bath, 6th October, 1814

Read a little more

It was addressed to me in a neat and careful script; the sort of hand, I think, that belongs to a person of patience and seriousness. It bore only a plain seal, with neither crest nor initial to reveal the writer.

I looked at it for some time before opening it, being equally curious and unwilling to bring the mystery too quickly to an end. At last I gave way to temptation and opened it at the breakfast table, whilst Mama was occupied with her toast and Edmund with the newspaper.

A few things you might wonder

What is a story letter subscription?

It is a fictional story told through physical letters posted to you. Love Letter Tales sends two letters a month, so you can follow the characters’ lives as their correspondence unfolds.

Is this a suitable gift for Mum or Grandma?

If she enjoys historical fiction, period dramas or beautiful stationery, it is a thoughtful way to give her something to look forward to. Choose a Regency romance with Rose Ashworth or wartime Britain with Clara Wren.

How much does it cost?

UK subscriptions start at £12.99 a month. UK gift plans cost £34.99 for 3 months, £64.99 for 6 months or £109.99 for 12 months, with UK postage included. See all prices and international options.

When are the letters posted?

Letters are posted on the 1st and 15th of each month. Delivery times depend on the destination. Contact us if you need help with a gift date.
