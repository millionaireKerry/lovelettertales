Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what happens to it. Written to be readable rather than impressive.

1. Who we are

ADHDementia provides screening, training and consultancy for care homes supporting people living with dementia who also have ADHD. For the purposes of UK data protection law, the data controller is [ADHDementia Ltd, company number 00000000, registered address], trading as ADHDementia.

If you have any question about this policy or about your personal data, contact us at [your email address] or through our contact page.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration number [ICO registration number].

2. The short version

  • The screening tool does not send or store any answers. Everything stays in your browser on your device.
  • The online course saves your name and progress in your own browser only, so that you can resume. We do not receive it.
  • The contact form sends us your details so that we can reply to you. That is all we use them for.
  • We do not sell your data, ever, to anyone.

3. The screening tool

The screening tool runs entirely in your web browser. The answers you give, the name or initials you enter, and the report the tool produces are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. We do not receive them, we cannot see them, and we do not store them.

This means you remain responsible for any report you save, print or share. If you complete a screening about a resident or a family member, the resulting report is personal information about that person, and your organisation's own data protection policies apply to how you store and share it.

Your responsibilities when screening someone else

  • Wherever possible, complete the screening with the person, and explain what it is for.
  • Store completed reports in the same way you would store any other care record.
  • Remember that the tool is not diagnostic and its output should not be recorded as a diagnosis.

4. The online course

The course stores the learner name you enter and your module progress in your browser's local storage, on your device. This is what allows you to leave the course and resume where you left off. It is not sent to us.

Certificates are generated in your browser and printed or saved by you. We do not hold a copy. [If you later issue certificates centrally or verify certificate IDs, this section will need updating.]

You can clear this stored information at any time by clearing your browser data for this site.

5. The contact form

When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, organisation (if you give one), your area of interest and your message. We use this only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to provide the services you have asked about.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services, and, where you become a customer, performance of a contract.

Retention: enquiry correspondence is kept for up to two years from our last contact, unless you become a customer, in which case business records are kept for six years to meet accounting and tax requirements.

Form submissions are processed by our website host, [Netlify, or your chosen host], which acts as a data processor on our behalf.

6. Subscriptions and payments

[This section applies once paid subscriptions and guide purchases are live.] When you subscribe or purchase, payment is handled by our payment processor, [Stripe]. We do not receive or store your full card details. We receive your name, email address, billing details and a record of your transaction, which we use to provide the service, issue invoices and meet our accounting obligations.

7. Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track you across other websites.

The screening tool and the online course use your browser's local storage to hold your progress, as described above. This is essential to how those tools work and stores nothing on our servers.

8. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us operate, and only as far as necessary:

  • our website host and form provider;
  • our payment processor, once payments are live;
  • our email provider;
  • our accountant, for business records.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to do so. We never sell personal data.

9. International transfers

Some of our service providers operate outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards those providers have in place, such as UK International Data Transfer Agreements or adequacy regulations.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to: be informed about how your data is used; request a copy of your data; have inaccurate data corrected; request erasure; restrict or object to processing; and request data portability. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

11. Children

Our services are intended for care professionals, families and adults. The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service develops, particularly when subscriptions and analytics go live. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
