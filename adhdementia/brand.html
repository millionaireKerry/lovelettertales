Brand kit

The ADHDementia brand

Everything needed to keep the brand consistent across the website, training slides, guides, social posts and documents.

The idea behind the name

ADHDementia is two words sharing a letter. ADHD and Dementia overlap on the D, which is exactly what the business is about: the population sitting in the intersection of two conditions, seen by almost nobody.

That overlap is the whole brand. The wordmark holds the two words together, and the icon draws the intersection literally. Everything else, the restraint, the gold, the serif, exists to make a serious clinical subject feel dignified rather than clinical.

Logo

The wordmark is the primary asset. "ADHD" is set in the darkest ink or white, and "ementia" in gold, so both words are readable inside one.

Clear space and minimum size

Leave clear space around the logo equal to the height of the capital A on all sides. Never place it on a busy photograph. Minimum width: 120px on screen, 30mm in print. Below that, use the icon alone.

Colour

Near-black and white do the work. Gold is the accent and should stay an accent, roughly one tenth of any layout. Teal is a quiet secondary for reassurance: correct answers, calm notices, supportive messages.

Accessibility

Gold on white does not pass contrast requirements for body text. Use gold for large headings, rules and accents only, never for small text on a light background. On dark backgrounds use Gold Bright.

Typography

Two typefaces, both free on Google Fonts. Playfair Display carries authority and warmth; Inter keeps everything else quietly readable.

Headings: Playfair Display
How many of your residents
have ADHD and don't know it?
Weight 700 for headings. Italic 600 in gold for the emphasised phrase, used once per heading at most. Tight line height (1.1 to 1.2).
Body: Inter
Today's care home generation grew up before ADHD had a name. They were called lazy, scatty, highly strung. They were never assessed.
Weight 400 for body, 600 for emphasis and buttons. Line height 1.6 to 1.7. Small capitals with wide letter spacing (0.2em) for the eyebrow labels above headings.

If the fonts are unavailable

In Word, PowerPoint or Canva, substitute Georgia for Playfair Display and Arial or Helvetica for Inter. Never substitute a rounded or handwritten face.

Voice

The voice is the brand's biggest asset, because the subject is emotive and the temptation to overclaim is constant. Resist it. Understatement reads as expertise.

Specific, not general

Name the cardigan, the dining room, the 3pm appointment. Concrete detail is what makes a care manager recognise their own resident.

"The cardigan is not a battle to be won. It is information."

Honest about evidence

Say association, not proof. State limitations before someone else does. Credibility here is worth more than certainty.

"These are associations rather than certainties, but the direction of the evidence is consistent."

Never diagnostic

The tool screens, it does not diagnose. Every piece of published material repeats this, without exception.

"The goal is not a label. It is a care plan that fits the person who actually lives there."

On the side of the staff

Care teams are doing their best inside systems that were not built for this. Reframe practice, never blame the carer.

"The housekeeper kindly tidies everything away. The room looks lovely. The resident is now lost in it."

House style

Always
  • UK English spelling: behaviour, recognised, person-centred, organisation
  • Commas, colons or brackets instead of long dashes
  • "People living with dementia", not "dementia patients" or "sufferers"
  • "Traits", not "symptoms", when describing lifelong ADHD patterns
  • Sentence case for headings, not Title Case
  • ADHDementia as one word, capital ADHD, lowercase ementia
Never
  • Claims of accreditation before it is granted
  • "Diagnose", "test", "assessment" for what the tool does
  • "Challenging behaviour" without quotation marks or reframing
  • Exclamation marks, or urgency language in marketing
  • Stock photography of sad older people looking out of windows
  • ADHDementia written as ADHD Dementia, ADHDemensure or Adhdementia

Applying the brand

Training slides

Dark cover slide with the stacked logo. Content slides on white or Paper, one idea per slide, Playfair headings and Inter body. Use the gold Key Point panel sparingly, no more than once per module. Photographs of environments, never of identifiable residents.

Guides and documents

Light background, primary logo top left, gold rule under the title, generous margins. Footer carries the non-diagnostic line on every document.

Social posts

Quote cards on Ink with the phrase in Playfair and the logo small in the corner. Leave plenty of space. One idea per card.

Email signature

Name, then "Founder, ADHDementia", then the descriptor "ADHD in dementia care", then adhdementia.com. Keep it text only, or add the icon at 60px.
