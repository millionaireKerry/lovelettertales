Why this training is different

The gap between compliant and exceptional

Your mandatory Oliver McGowan training covers autism and learning disability, because that is what the Health and Care Act 2022 names. It does not teach your team what rejection sensitive dysphoria looks like in an eighty-year-old woman, why a resident with time blindness is paralysed all day by a 3pm appointment, or how the erosion of lifelong masking in early dementia can be mistaken for rapid decline.

ADHDementia training does. It is built on a specialist screening framework for older adults, informed by doctoral research, and grounded in the reality of care home life: rotas, dining rooms, medication rounds and handover notes. Your team leaves seeing residents differently, and knowing exactly what to change on their next shift.
