Online course

ADHD Awareness in Dementia Care

An interactive course for care home staff. About one hour, six modules, a short assessment, and a certificate at the end.

Welcome

This course introduces the essentials of recognising and supporting ADHD traits in older adults living with dementia. It is designed for all care home staff, from carers and nurses to housekeeping, kitchen and activities teams, because every role shapes a resident's day. Work through the six modules in order, answer the knowledge checks as you go, and pass a short final assessment to receive your certificate.

By the end of this course you will be able to
  • Explain why many older adults have ADHD that was never diagnosed
  • Recognise common ADHD traits and how they present in dementia care
  • Reframe "challenging behaviour" as possible unmet neurodivergent need
  • Apply practical adjustments in your own role, on your next shift
  • Understand how screening supports person-centred care planning
