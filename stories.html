Regency Romance

Eleanor Ashford

Bath, 1812. Eleanor Ashford is sharp, restless, and entirely unsuited to the life her family has planned for her. When a mysterious stranger arrives at the Assembly Rooms, her carefully ordered world begins to unravel — one letter at a time.

A tale of longing, wit, and quiet rebellion against the expectations of Regency society. Fans of Jane Austen and Bridgerton will feel right at home.

Wartime Britain

Violet Wren

London, 1942. Violet Wren is a young nurse working through the Blitz, writing letters to a soldier she barely knows — and slowly falling in love with his words. But the war has secrets of its own, and not every letter tells the whole truth.

A story of courage, heartbreak, and the power of connection in the darkest of times. For readers who love The Guernsey Literary and Potato Peel Pie Society.

Victorian Mystery

Clara Nightingale

Yorkshire, 1889. Clara Nightingale arrives at Thornfield House as the new governess — only to discover a bundle of unsent letters hidden behind a loose stone in the nursery wall. The letters reveal a decades-old mystery that the household would prefer stayed buried.

A gothic tale of secrets, loyalty, and the things we hide in plain sight. Perfect for fans of Sarah Waters and the Brontës.
